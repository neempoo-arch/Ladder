The short version

The numbers you type — your rent, your debts, your savings — stay on your device. They are never sent to us. The math runs inside your browser.

If you ask for email reminders, we store your email address and which step you're on. Nothing else.

We don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we don't connect to your bank.

Where your information actually lives

InformationWhere it goes
Your bills, rent, debts, savings, and every figure you enter Your device only. Held in your browser while you use the page, and written to a file only if you press Save my file. That file is downloaded to your device. We never receive it.
Your email address Our servers — but only if you choose to sign up for reminders. Stored alongside which step you're on and your reminder preference.
Page visits We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without building a profile of you. We see totals, not people.
Technical logs Our host records standard request information (IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. Retained briefly, not linked to anything you typed.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone
  • Show you advertising, or let advertisers target you through us
  • Connect to your bank or ask for banking credentials
  • Ask for your Social Security number, account numbers, or date of birth
  • Share what you entered with a partner, lender, or affiliate
  • Charge you for something you didn't clearly agree to

Why we built it this way

Financial information is sensitive, and the safest way to protect it is not to hold it. If we don't have your numbers, they can't be leaked, subpoenaed, sold, or lost in a breach.

The trade-off is honest: because we don't store your data, we can't sync it between your phone and laptop, and we can't recover it if you lose your file. That's why the Save my file button exists. Keep the file somewhere you'll find it.

Your file

When you save, your browser downloads a small file to your device. It's plain, readable JSON — you can open it in any text editor and see exactly what it contains.

That file is yours. Copy it, back it up, or delete it. Deleting it removes your information completely, because there is no other copy.

Email reminders

If you sign up, we store your email address, which step you're on, and how often you want to hear from us. We use that only to send the reminders you asked for.

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes your address from our records. You can also email us at [EMAIL] to be removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how it's used.

In practice this is simple here: unless you signed up for email, we hold nothing about you. If you did, email [EMAIL] and we'll show you the record or delete it. We'll respond within 30 days and won't charge you or treat you differently for asking.

Age

This site is for adults. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us an email address, contact us and we'll remove it.

Where this is available

The site is intended for people in the United States. Figures are indexed to U.S. price data and the guidance reflects U.S. accounts and terminology.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant and you're on our email list, we'll tell you directly rather than quietly editing the page.
