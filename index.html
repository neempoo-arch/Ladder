Five steps. One at a time.

Each one pairs something calm with something practical. The calm part is free and takes ten seconds. Start there when the money part feels like too much.

1 Take a breath. Put aside a small pile of cash, sized to your actual bills and today's prices. Ready now
2 Drink some water. Find the debt hurting you most — not the biggest one, the one taking the most from your month.
3 Stand up and stretch. Build the real cushion. Step 1 was for a flat tire. This one is for losing your job.
4 Find one good thing today. Check whether your money is working or leaking. What savings earns, against what debt costs.
5 Do something that's yours. You're safe now. This is where money starts making money.
